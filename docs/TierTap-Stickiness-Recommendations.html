Executive Summary

Where TierTap Stands Today

TierTap

Strengths (In-Session)

  • Live Activity & Apple Watch sync during play
  • Fast check-in presets for repeat visits
  • Tap Level progression (1–1,000) with celebrations
  • Play Reminders during live sessions
  • Post-closeout confetti, session art & community publish
  • Wallet tier sync toast at close-out

Gaps (Between Sessions)

  • No guided onboarding — splash → optional auth → Check In
  • No logging streaks or consistency rewards
  • No off-session push notifications or home screen widget
  • History buried in a sheet; no journal-style tab
  • Tier goals ephemeral — no persistent progress tracker
  • Trips lack pre-trip prep and post-trip recap hooks
  • Analytics empty until many sessions; insights are pull-only

Core Opportunity

The biggest retention upside is between-session value — trip bookends, tier progress, monthly recaps, widgets, and a browsable journal — while keeping responsible-gaming framing (reward tracking and awareness, not more play). Apps like Strava, Duolingo, and Apple Fitness succeed by creating recurring reasons to open the app when the core activity isn't happening.

01
Activation

Guided Activation Onboarding

TierTap
Opportunity

New users land on a large logo and "Check In" with no guided path. Wallet, bankroll, fast check-in, and analytics stay undiscovered unless users dig.

Recommendation

Add a lightweight 3–5 step activation checklist on Home until complete:

  • Link a wallet card
  • Set bankroll
  • Log first session & close out with tier points
  • Configure one fast check-in preset
Effort: Medium
Inspiration

Duolingo first lesson, YNAB setup wizard, Notion "Get started" checklist — all optimize for time-to-first-win, not feature tours.

Why It Sticks

Users who complete one full session loop are far more likely to return. A checklist turns abstract value ("track tier points") into concrete, completable actions.

Habit Loop

Tracking Consistency Streaks

TierTap
Opportunity

Tap Level rewards logging consistency and AI prompts mention streaks — but no visible streak system exists. Nothing celebrates "you tracked every session on your last 3 trips."

Recommendation

Introduce logging streaks: consecutive casino visits with at least one logged session, or consecutive weeks with any session. Surface on the Tap Level card with a streak indicator. Optional gentle nudge before a planned trip.

Effort: Low–Medium
Inspiration

Duolingo streaks, Apple Fitness "Close your rings," Strava "week active." Frame as discipline and record-keeping — not play frequency.

Why It Sticks

Streaks are among the strongest habit loops in consumer apps — as long as they reward tracking, not gambling more.

03
Loyalty

Persistent Loyalty Tier Goal Tracker

TierTap
Opportunity

Wallet tier sync shows a brief toast at close-out, then disappears. There is no always-visible "X points to next tier" progress anywhere in the app.

Recommendation

Add a tier goal ring on Home and in Wallet: current points, next tier threshold, projected date at recent pace. Before trips: "At your usual pace, you'll reach [tier] by day 3 of Vegas."

Effort: Medium
Inspiration

Apple Fitness activity rings, Chase/Amex rewards trackers, airline status progress bars.

Why It Sticks

Tier progression is a core motivator for casino players. Making it ambient — not buried in close-out — gives a reason to open the app even when not playing.

TierTap Stickiness Recommendations6
04
Events

Trip-Mode: Pre-Trip Prep & Post-Trip Recap

TierTap
Opportunity

Trips exist with AI suggestions, but no proactive hooks. Upcoming trips don't surface on Home; there's no post-trip summary or shareable recap.

Recommendation
  • Pre-trip (7 days out): Home card with countdown, fast check-in presets for that property, bankroll reminder, linked wallet cards
  • Post-trip: Auto-generated recap — sessions, P&L, tier points, best/worst session, photo highlights. Shareable like session art.
Effort: Medium
Inspiration

TripIt pre-trip alerts, Spotify Wrapped, Strava "Your week in sport." Casino trips are emotional peaks — TierTap should bookend them.

Why It Sticks

Trips are natural re-engagement anchors: plan → play → reminisce. Three distinct open-the-app moments per trip.

05
Re-engagement

Between-Session Notifications

TierTap
Opportunity

Notifications today are in-session only (Play Reminders). Nothing pulls users back when they're home between casino visits.

Recommendation

Add opt-in, value-driven pushes:

  • Monthly recap ready
  • Trip starts tomorrow
  • Incomplete session needing close-out (Watch → iPhone)
  • Tier milestone within reach
  • Soft monthly check-in if no sessions logged
Effort: Medium
Inspiration

Strava weekly summaries, Apple Health monthly trends, banking apps ("Your statement is ready").

Why It Sticks

Most users aren't in a live session most of the time. Off-session touchpoints create habitual check-ins without requiring a casino visit.

Presence

iOS Home Screen Widget (Off-Session)

TierTap
Opportunity

Live Activity and Watch complications work during play. There is no home screen presence when idle — no passive reminder that TierTap holds your data.

Recommendation

Small/medium widgets showing: Tap Level + progress bar, next trip countdown, YTD tier points, last session result, or "Ready to check in?" with fast-check-in deep link.

Effort: Medium
Inspiration

Robinhood portfolio glance, weather/calendar widgets, Strava weekly mileage widget.

Why It Sticks

Widgets are passive billboards. Every phone unlock is a micro-reminder — without opening the app.

07
Retention

Elevate History + Photo Feed to a Tab

TierTap
Opportunity

History is a sheet from Home; photo feed is a toolbar icon. MainTab.history exists in code but isn't wired to the tab bar. Past sessions are hard to browse.

Recommendation

Replace or supplement a tab with Journal (History + Photo Feed + trip-linked sessions). Make revisiting past play as easy as starting a new session.

Effort: Low (mostly navigation)
Inspiration

Day One journal, Apple Photos Memories, Strava activity feed. Emotional recall drives retention more than utility features.

Why It Sticks

Users who build a rich history have high switching costs. A journal tab turns TierTap from a utility into a personal casino diary.

08
Calendar Moment

Monthly "TierTap Wrapped" Insight Cards

TierTap
Opportunity

Analytics is powerful but empty until many sessions exist. Insights are pull-only; Pro AI analysis requires user initiation and a paywall.

Recommendation

Auto-generate a monthly insight card on the 1st: sessions played, tier points earned, most efficient game, mood trend, biggest win/loss. Shareable image. Free = basic card; Pro = AI narrative summary.

Effort: Medium
Inspiration

Spotify Wrapped, Apple Music Replay, Strava Year in Sport, Monzo/Revolut year-in-review.

Why It Sticks

Creates a recurring calendar moment to open the app even in slow play months. Shareability drives acquisition loops too.

09
Data Quality

Close-Out Completeness Score

TierTap
Opportunity

Tap Level scoring rewards tier gains and rated/actual bet alignment, but users don't know incomplete close-outs cost progression. History has verified vs. unverified tier tabs — data quality is a known issue.

Recommendation

At close-out and on incomplete sessions, show: "+15 Tap Level progress available if you add ending tier + avg bet." A profile-strength-style meter on Home for data completeness.

Effort: Low–Medium
Inspiration

LinkedIn profile strength, Apple Watch activity rings "close your move ring," fitness apps' form-quality scores.

Why It Sticks

Turns data entry from chore into game mechanic. Better data → better analytics → more reason to return and explore insights.

TierTap Stickiness Recommendations12
10
Streamlining

Smart Home + Reduce Auth & Social Friction

TierTap
Opportunity

Returning users with presets still see a large logo and generic "Check In." Auth is offered once per launch when unsigned in. Community is Pro-gated with an often-empty feed.

Recommendation
  • Smart Home: If fast check-in presets exist, make the preset bar the primary CTA; demote logo on repeat visits
  • Auth: Prompt after value (e.g., 3rd session or first trip), not every cold launch
  • Community: Read-only free feed or trip-buddy co-tracking; story-ready session art templates with deep links
Effort: Low–High (varies)
Inspiration

Uber recent destinations home, Instagram share-to-stories flow, Splitwise trip groups.

Why It Sticks

Every tap removed from check-in increases use at the high-intent moment (sitting at a table). Social and share loops turn solo tracking into identity and community.
